Pick a project that interests you. Read its overview and readiness check before you begin. Both current workshops are Creator level because they use variables; Catch the apples introduces them with smaller steps.

| Workshop | Level | Time | What you will make |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Catch the apples](catch-game/) | Creator | About 65 minutes | An arrow-controlled catch game with a score. Includes a warm-up for the entry skills. |
| [Memory match](memory-match/) | Creator | About 100 minutes | A shuffled card game using variables, lists, clones, and messages. |

The time estimates include building and debugging. Optional challenges and warm-ups may take longer. No account is needed; save your work on your computer.

New to coding? [Read the level guide](../#choose-your-level) and [find out about Scratch](../#what-is-scratch).
