Make something, test it, and make it your own.

## Memory match

Build eight hidden cards. Turn over two, find a pair, and keep going until you have all four matches.

**Creator level · ages 8–15 · about 100 minutes**  
Split the workshop into two sessions if you like. An adult or coding buddy can help you read the longer scripts.

[Start the memory-match workshop →](workshops/memory-match/)

You will use Scratch 3. You can save on your device without making an account.
